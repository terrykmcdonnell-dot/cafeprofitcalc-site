Whether you've spotted a number that looks off, have a question about how the calculator works, or just want to say the tool was useful — we'd like to hear from you.

Email

The fastest way to reach us is hello@cafeprofitcalc.com. We read every message, including corrections to the benchmark figures — see the About page for where those numbers come from.

What to include

If you're reporting a benchmark that looks wrong, it helps to tell us your market (country or region) and café concept (coffee-forward vs full food), since the ranges we publish vary by both. If something on the calculator itself isn't working as expected, a screenshot and the browser you're using goes a long way.
